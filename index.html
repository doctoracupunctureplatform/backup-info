Doctor Acupuncture Backup

A private backup utility operated by the Doctor Acupuncture Platform account owner. It runs on the owner's Mac and uploads database and media backup archives to the owner's Google Drive.

Google Drive access is used to create backup files, check their integrity, and move eligible older backups to Trash under the configured retention rule. Deleting a local source archive does not request deletion of the cloud copy.

This utility is for the account owner's use. It is not a patient-facing service and does not provide medical advice.
