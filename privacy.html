Privacy information — Doctor Acupuncture Backup

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

Purpose and operation

This private utility runs on the account owner's Mac. It uploads backup archives selected by the owner to the owner's Google Drive for recovery of the Doctor Acupuncture Platform.

Google data access

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. It uses access to files created by or explicitly authorized for this application to upload backups, inspect file names, sizes and checksums, and manage backup retention. It does not request access to all other files in Google Drive.

Storage and disclosure

Backup archives are stored on the owner's Mac and in the Google Drive account authorized by the owner. Google processes uploaded files as the storage provider. The utility does not send backup contents to this website, advertising services, analytics services, or AI models. OAuth credentials are stored locally for unattended uploads and are excluded from the backup archives.

Retention and control

The configured retention period is 365 days. Cleanup preserves the most recent verified backup even when it is older than that period. Eligible expired cloud archives are moved to Google Drive Trash. Unknown or changed files are preserved when the utility cannot validate them. The utility does not empty Drive Trash; Google Drive's own Trash rules apply.

The owner can stop the scheduled job, revoke the application's access in Google Account settings, or delete cloud files directly in Google Drive. Revoking access or deleting local source files does not itself delete previously uploaded backups.
